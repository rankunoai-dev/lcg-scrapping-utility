Welcome to Our School

Welcome to AppleTree Kentwood, MI! Our educational childcare and preschool center is conveniently located just one mile from US-131, making us a trusted choice for families across Kentwood, Wyoming, and Byron Center.

We offer a full range of programs, including Infants, Toddlers, Twos, Preschool, Pre-K, and School-Age care, with flexible options such as full-time, part-time, drop-in care, and Summer Camp. We also partner with the state of Michigan to provide the Great Start Readiness Program (GSRP), a free, state-funded Pre-K program that helps prepare children for kindergarten success. Children can also enjoy engaging enrichment opportunities, including Spanish and Soccer.

Our dedicated staff brings over 60 years of combined experience and is fully CPR and First Aid certified. Every day is filled with hands-on learning experiences that spark curiosity, creativity, and confidence. We also provide a variety of healthy meal and snack options and encourage children to explore new foods.

To keep families informed and connected, we offer live-streaming classroom video and real-time updates through our exclusive family app, SproutAbout.

Schedule a tour today to experience our highly trusted daycare, childcare, and preschool firsthand. Visit us at 732 52nd St. SE, Kentwood, MI 49548.
