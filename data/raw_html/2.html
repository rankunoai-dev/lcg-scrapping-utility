Welcome to Our School

Welcome to Childtime located in Fairport, NY!

At Childtime, our unique curriculum begins in our Infant program and progresses with your child as they advance through into our various early education programs, including Preschool, Pre-Kindergarten and more. We have a wonderful group of teachers with more than 45 years of combined experience. They are continuously educating themselves to be the best of the best with monthly trainings and staff development training days.

We are located off Garnsey Road in a quiet neighborhood which allows us to offer a calm, loving experience for the children in our care. We are dedicated to our families and focused on creating a home away from home for everyone who steps into our building. We strongly believe that open communication with families is the key to success for all of our children.

We’re committed to keeping you connected throughout the day while your child is in our care. Get access to live streaming video of your child’s classroom, plus other real-time updates, with our exclusive mobile app for families, SproutAbout.

If you are looking for a nurturing, educational daycare in the Fairport, Victor, or Pittsford area, then give our Childtime a call today!
