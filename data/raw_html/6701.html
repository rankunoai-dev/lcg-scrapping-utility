Welcome to Our School

Welcome to Creative Kids – Craig Road, where we provide high-quality daycare, preschool, and early education in a safe, nurturing environment for children to learn, grow, and thrive. Our educational child care programs serve infants beginning at 6 weeks, toddlers, preschoolers, pre-kindergarteners, and school-age children through our exciting Summer Camp program.

Our school is proudly star-rated through Nevada's Quality Rating & Improvement System (QRIS) and nationally accredited by the National Early Childhood Program Accreditation (NECPA), reflecting our commitment to providing exceptional early childhood education. Families can feel confident knowing our experienced team brings more than 120 years of combined experience and is dedicated to helping every child reach their full potential.

Our teachers complete a minimum of 30 hours of professional development each year and maintain certifications in CPR, First Aid, SIDS, Health and Wellness, and Positive Guidance. Their expertise and dedication create a warm, engaging learning environment where children build confidence, independence, and a lifelong love of learning.

We know how important it is to stay connected throughout your child's day. Through our exclusive SproutAbout® app, families receive live streaming classroom video, real-time updates, photos, and classroom communication, providing added peace of mind while their child is in our care.

Schedule a tour today to discover why families throughout the Las Vegas and North Las Vegas communities choose Creative Kids – Craig Road for trusted daycare, preschool, and early education. We look forward to welcoming your family!
